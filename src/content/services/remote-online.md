Secure remote online notarization via audio-video technology:

- No need to meet in person
- Identity verification through knowledge-based authentication
- Secure encrypted platform
- Session recording for compliance
- Available for eligible document types under Florida law

Remote online notarization combines convenience with security, allowing you to complete notarizations from anywhere with an internet connection.

**Requirements:**
- Valid government-issued ID
- Webcam and microphone
- Stable internet connection
