We provide professional notarization for all types of documents:

- Affidavits and sworn statements
- Power of attorney documents
- Wills and living wills
- Contracts and agreements
- Deeds and titles
- Medical documents and HIPAA forms
- Passport application documents
- Immigration documents (I-9 verification available)
- Court documents
- Financial documents

All notarizations are performed by a Florida-commissioned notary public with proper identification verification and recordkeeping.
