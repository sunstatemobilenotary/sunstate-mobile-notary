---
question: What documents can't you notarize?
category: General
order: 7
---

Notaries cannot:

- Notarize incomplete documents
- Notarize documents where the signer cannot be identified
- Notarize for someone who appears coerced or lacks understanding
- Notarize documents in which they have a personal interest
- Provide legal advice or draft legal documents (unless also an attorney)
- Certify copies of birth certificates, marriage certificates, or other vital records (these require certified copies from the issuing agency)

When in doubt, contact us and we'll let you know if your document can be notarized.
