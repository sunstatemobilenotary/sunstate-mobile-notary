category: Booking
order: 4
---

You can schedule an appointment by:

1. **Booking online** - Use our [booking form](/book) to request a date and time
2. **Calling or texting** - Reach us directly for immediate scheduling
3. **Email** - Send your request to rich@sunstatemobilenotary.com

We offer same-day and next-day appointments based on availability, and emergency/after-hours service by special arrangement.
