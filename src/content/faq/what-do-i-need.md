---
question: What do I need to bring to get something notarized?
category: General
order: 2
---

You need:

1. **Valid government-issued photo ID** - Driver's license, passport, state ID, or military ID
2. **The document to be notarized** - Unsigned (you'll sign in front of the notary)
3. **All signers present** - Everyone who needs to sign must appear in person with their own ID
4. **Payment** - Cash, card, or agreed-upon payment method

Do not sign the document beforehand - you must sign in the presence of the notary.
