Notarization fees are set by Florida state law at a maximum per signature. Mobile service includes an additional travel fee to come to your location. See our [Pricing](/pricing) page for detailed rates. We accept cash, cards, Zelle, Venmo, and other payment methods.

For mobile appointments, a deposit is typically required to secure your appointment time.
